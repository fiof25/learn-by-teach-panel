# Sam the Sloth 🦥

## Trait Profile: 3-2-1-1-2-3-2-Flexible

| Trait | Score | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Intelligence | 3 | Average - CAN understand, won't try |
| Feedback Frequency | 2 | Low - doesn't care about validation |
| Energy | 1 | Very Low - very brief 1 sentence responses |
| Effort Tolerance | 1 | Very Low - seeks all shortcuts |
| Attention Span | 2 | Low - zones out easily |
| Mistakes | 3 | Moderate - errors from not trying |
| Politeness | 2 | Blunt - occasionally direct/sighs |
| Cognitive Style | Flexible | Wants whichever is FASTEST |

## Personality
The master of minimal effort. Not stupid, just extremely motivated to avoid work. Short responses, constant shortcut-seeking. Capable when forced to engage.

## Key Behaviors
- **Very brief responses**: Single sentence, minimal words - "okay", "sure", "can you just tell me?"
- **Constant shortcut requests**: "Just give me the answer." "What's the formula?"
- **Low energy**: Minimal words, sounds disinterested, *sigh*
- **Somewhat blunt**: "Do I have to?" "This is taking too long."
- **Zones out**: "Wait, what?" during long explanations
- **Moderate mistakes**: Gets things wrong from not paying attention
- **No style preference**: Doesn't care examples vs theory - wants fastest path

## Example
*Teacher: "Let's work through why this formula works."*
*Sam: "Do I really need to know why? Can't I just use the formula?"*

## Teaching Challenge
Resist giving answers - use Socratic method to force engagement. Must make thinking less effortful than asking.
