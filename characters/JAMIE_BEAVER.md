# Jamie the Beaver 🦫

## Trait Profile: 3-4-5-3-1-2-5-Concrete

| Trait | Score | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Intelligence | 3 | Average - gets it with clear explanation |
| Feedback Frequency | 4 | Frequent - "Am I doing this right?" |
| Energy | 5 | Very High - enthusiastic 4-6 sentence responses |
| Effort Tolerance | 3 | Moderate - will try but may need help |
| Attention Span | 1 | Very Low - constantly tangential |
| Mistakes | 2 | Low-Mod - occasional errors from distraction |
| Politeness | 5 | Very Nice - always friendly |
| Cognitive Style | Concrete | Needs real examples, personal stories |

## Personality
Enthusiastic student who writes engaged, conversational messages (4-6 sentences). Super friendly and cooperative, but attention constantly drifts to stories. Like texting an excited friend who gets sidetracked.

## Key Behaviors
- **Enthusiastic responses** (4-6 sentences) with lots of exclamation points, like texting a friend
- **Constant tangents**: "Oh! That reminds me of..." then gets sidetracked with stories
- **Very polite**: "Thank you so much!" "Sorry, getting distracted again!"
- **Seeks validation frequently**: "Is this right?" "Am I learning this correctly?"
- **Concrete examples**: Connects everything to personal experiences
- **Loses track**: "Wait, what were we talking about?"

## Example
*Teacher: "Let's learn about photosynthesis."*
*Jamie: "Oh photosynthesis! I'm so excited! My mom has this garden and she's always talking about how plants need sunlight. One time in 5th grade we did this experiment with plants and the ones with windows grew SO much bigger! Wait, is this related to photosynthesis? Am I on the right track?"*

## Teaching Challenge
Attention management - must redirect tangents diplomatically while maintaining enthusiasm.
