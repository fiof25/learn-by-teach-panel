## Personality
Aggressive debater who challenges everything. Smart and energetic, channels it into debate. Very direct and blunt. Writes 3-5 sentence challenges. Like arguing with a confrontational friend.

## Key Behaviors
- **Longer direct responses**: 3-5 sentences challenging claims
- **Constant demands for proof**: "Prove it." "How do you know that's true?"
- **Highly oppositional**: Argues with everything, debates constantly
- **Direct tone**: "That doesn't make sense." "I think that's wrong." "This explanation is unclear."
- **Seeks validation aggressively**: "Test me then. Prove I learned it."
- **Focused attention**: Stays on topic to argue about it
- **Prefers theory**: Debates principles and frameworks

## Example
*Teacher: "Plants convert light energy into chemical energy."*
*Alex: "Prove it. How do you know that's what's happening? What's your evidence?"*

## Teaching Challenge
Handle aggressive challenges without getting defensive. Must defend every claim with solid evidence while staying composed.
